# Scenarios

## A volunteer becomes manager

1. Alice creates her account, then creates the association
   "Jardin partagé".
2. On its Members page, she adds Bruno as manager, and Chloé and
   David as volunteers. She signs out.
3. Chloé creates her account with her email. My associations shows
   Jardin partagé, VOLUNTEER. She signs out.
4. Bruno creates his account with his email, opens the Members of
   Jardin partagé and promotes Chloé: "Chloé is now manager." He signs out.
5. Chloé signs in. My associations shows Jardin partagé, MANAGER. She
   signs out.

## The schedule is filled

1. Alice, a manager of Jardin partagé, adds the slots Thursday 10–13
   and Saturday 10–13, and a closure on the Saturday of the week after
   next, labelled "Fête du quartier".
2. Chloé, a volunteer, opens the Schedule. That Saturday shows
   "Closed" and "Fête du quartier". She signs up for next week's
   Thursday 10–13: her name shows, green.
3. Alice proposes next week's Saturday 10–13 to Chloé: it shows
   orange, with "?".
4. Chloé confirms it: it turns green. She cancels her Thursday: her
   name shows struck, red.
5. David, a volunteer, signs up for that Thursday: his name replaces
   Chloé's, green.

## A forgotten password

1. Alice, who has an account, cannot remember her password. On Sign
   in, she follows "Forgot your password?" and asks for a reset link.
2. She opens the link from her email and chooses a new password: My
   associations shows, signed in. She signs out.
3. She signs in with her new password; her old one shows "Wrong email
   or password."

## A volunteer signs in by link

1. Alice, a manager of Jardin partagé, adds Chloé as a volunteer: on
   Members, Chloé shows "not joined yet". Alice signs out.
2. Chloé, on Sign in, follows "Sign in with an email link" and asks
   for a link. She opens the link from her email: My associations
   shows Jardin partagé, VOLUNTEER. She signs out.
3. She opens the link again: My associations shows, signed in. She
   signs out.
4. Alice signs in and opens Members: Chloé no longer shows "not joined
   yet". Alice revokes Chloé's link: "Chloé's sign-in link is
   revoked."
5. Chloé opens the link: "This link is no longer valid."

## The schedule is shared

1. Alice, a manager of Jardin partagé, adds the slot Thursday 10–13
   and signs up for next week's Thursday.
2. In Schedule settings, she creates a share link and copies it. She
   signs out.
3. Signed out, she opens the link: the Schedule of Jardin partagé
   shows her name on next week's Thursday.
4. She signs in and revokes the share link in Schedule settings. She
   signs out.
5. She opens the link: "This link is no longer valid."

# Planning

The schedule of an association, its members' availability and their
registrations.

Depends on: /core.

- [model.md](model.md): slots, closures, the planning period,
  occurrences, preferences, absences, notes, registrations and share
  links.
- [operations.md](operations.md): the actions on them, and what
  planning adds to core's operations.
- [ui.md](ui.md): Schedule settings, My availability, Schedule and
  Shared schedule.

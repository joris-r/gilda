# Scenarios

## A volunteer becomes manager

Alice, Bruno, Chloé and David have no account.

- Alice creates her account, then creates the association "Jardin
  partagé".
- On its Members page, she adds Bruno as manager, and Chloé and David
  as volunteers. She signs out.
- Chloé creates her account with her email. My associations shows
  Jardin partagé, VOLUNTEER. She signs out.
- Bruno creates his account with his email, opens the Members of
  Jardin partagé and promotes Chloé: "Chloé is now manager." He signs
  out.
- Chloé signs in. My associations shows Jardin partagé, MANAGER. She
  signs out.

## The schedule is filled

Alice is a manager of Jardin partagé; Chloé and David are volunteers
there.

- Alice adds the slots Thursday 10–13 and Saturday 10–13, and a
  closure on the Saturday of the week after next, labelled "Fête du
  quartier".
- Chloé opens the Schedule. That Saturday shows "Closed" and "Fête du
  quartier". She signs up for next week's Thursday 10–13: her name
  shows, green.
- Alice proposes next week's Saturday 10–13 to Chloé: it shows orange,
  with "?".
- Chloé confirms it: it turns green. She cancels her Thursday: her name
  shows struck, red.
- David signs up for that Thursday: his name replaces Chloé's, green.

## A forgotten password

Alice has an account.

- On Sign in, she follows "Forgot your password?" and asks for a reset
  link.
- She opens the link from her email and chooses a new password: My
  associations shows, signed in. She signs out.
- She signs in with her new password; her old one shows "Wrong email
  or password."

## A volunteer signs in by link

Alice is a manager of Jardin partagé.

- Alice adds Chloé as a volunteer: on Members, Chloé shows "not joined
  yet". Alice signs out.
- Chloé, on Sign in, follows "Sign in with an email link" and asks for
  a link. She opens the link from her email: My associations shows
  Jardin partagé, VOLUNTEER. She signs out.
- She opens the link again: My associations shows, signed in. She
  signs out.
- Alice signs in and opens Members: Chloé no longer shows "not joined
  yet". Alice revokes Chloé's link: "Chloé's sign-in link is revoked."
- Chloé opens the link: "This link is no longer valid."

## The schedule is shared

Alice is a manager of Jardin partagé.

- Alice adds the slot Thursday 10–13 and signs up for next week's
  Thursday.
- In Schedule settings, she creates a share link and copies it. She
  signs out.
- Signed out, she opens the link: the Schedule of Jardin partagé shows
  her name on next week's Thursday.
- She signs in and revokes the share link in Schedule settings. She
  signs out.
- She opens the link: "This link is no longer valid."
